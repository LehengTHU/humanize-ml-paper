## Unreleased

- `examples/sourcestein-icml2027/`: the one-shot ICML paper from the blog post, with its prompt, source, code and results.

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-21)

First public release, the version used in the blog post [The Art of Writing](https://lehengthu.github.io/blog/2026/paper-skill-ai-detection/).

- `skills/art-of-writing/`: the workflow from a proposal to a finished paper, 12 reference files, a phrase bank of 1,272 attested expressions, LaTeX templates for ICLR, NeurIPS and ICML, and `check_paper.py`, which reports word budgets, the page limit, required statements and 27 habits of machine-written drafts.
- Installable as a Claude Code plugin (`.claude-plugin/`), with `npx skills add` and with `gh skill install`.
